YOUR PERSONAL WALL — QUICK START

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

PHONE PREVIEW:
1. Extract the ZIP file on your Android phone.
2. Open the extracted folder in an HTML editor such as Acode.
3. Open index.html and use the app's Preview option.
4. Tap "Open my letter".
5. Use "Choose a picture" and "Choose music" to preview your own files.

IMPORTANT:
- The picture and music selectors in this first version are for preview on your device.
- To make your selected picture and song available to the recipient through a public link, the files must be added to the website folder before publishing.
- Music does not autoplay automatically; the recipient needs to press Play.
- Internet access is needed for the Google Fonts used here. The page still has fallback fonts if offline.

PUBLISHING:
When your files and chosen media are ready, upload the complete website folder (including your picture and audio file once we add them) to a static website host such as Netlify. Do not share private photos publicly unless you are comfortable with that.
